Each theme explores a different balance of color, contrast, and mood. Theme 1 is most Linear-like (monochrome, minimal). Themes 2-4 introduce more color personality while maintaining the dark, modern aesthetic. The weather gradients subtly reinforce YES/NO answers without being literal.
